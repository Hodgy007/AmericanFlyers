# Riding together

Everyone who is signed in and riding the same stage at the same time appears on the road with you, with their name above their head. You appear on their road too.

## How it works

- Sign in (free) with Google or your email. Signed-out riders ride alone.
- Pick a stage. Everyone on that stage, anywhere along it, is in the same ride.
- Riders within about 300 m behind and 900 m ahead are drawn on the road (up to 12 at once). Riders further away still count in the list.
- Names are shortened to first name and last initial, for example *Steve H.*

Your position is sent every 2 seconds. Between updates each rider moves on at their last speed, so they glide smoothly instead of jumping.

## The 👥 button

The 👥 button in the top bar shows how many other riders are on your stage. It glows green when anyone is there. Click it to open **Riding now**:

- Each rider's name, how far ahead or behind you they are, and their power and speed.
- **Ride with**: starts a new ride right next to that rider, so you can ride together. Your current ride is saved first if it was a minute or longer.
- **Copy link to this stage**: copies a link that opens the same stage. Send it to a friend, and you'll see each other once you're both riding.

When someone joins your stage, a message says so.

## Riding with a friend

1. Both sign in.
2. Open the same stage. Send the link from **Copy link to this stage**.
3. Start pedalling. If you're far apart on the road, open 👥 and press **Ride with** next to your friend's name.

## Pro teams

Five computer-ridden pro teams ride every stage with you, signed in or not. Each rides two by two in its own kit at a steady pace: 2.0, 2.6, 3.1, 3.6 and 4.2 W/kg, slowing on the climbs and flying down the descents like you do. The front rider's tag shows the team and its pace.

The team names are affectionate near-misses of the real ones (*Plasma | Lease-a-Trike*, *INEPTOS Grenadines*, *Scandal Quick-Slip*, *UAE Team Emigrates* and more). The riders are made up.

Open 👥 to see them under **Pro teams on the road**: how far ahead or behind each one is, its pace in watts for your weight, and its speed. **Join** starts a new ride on the back of the group, rolling at their speed, so you're straight into their draft. Hold their watts (a little less, thanks to the draft) and you'll stay with them.

On a stage with a finish, teams that cross the line start the stage again. Switch them off with **Pro teams on the road** in ⚙ Settings.

## Drafting

Ride close behind another rider and you're in their slipstream: you need fewer watts for the same speed, just like outside.

- About a third less air drag right on one rider's wheel, about a quarter at 2–3 m, fading to nothing by 12 m.
- **Behind a group it's more**: about 45% behind two riders, 50% behind three, and up to about 54% on the back of a pro team.
- The percentage is the same at any speed, but the watts it saves grow fast with speed: on one wheel about 130 W at 45 km/h, 25 W at 25 km/h, and almost nothing on a steep climb.
- With a smart trainer, the trainer gets lighter too.
- Ghosts don't make a draft; real riders and the pro teams do.
- On a steep climb you're slow, so drag (and the draft) matters much less than on the flat.

### The draft gauge

When a rider is within 60 m ahead, a gauge appears above the bottom of the screen:

- **"Marcus S. ahead · 28 m"** and *Close to 12 m to get in the draft*: the bar fills as you close in, and the blue end is the draft zone.
- **"In Marcus S.'s draft · 2.4 m"**, with *−21% drag · saving ~35 W*: you're in. Behind a group it says how many, for example *In Coffeedis's draft · 5 riders*. The bar turns green within 2 m, the sweet spot for the biggest saving.

Riders you're following move into your lane, so you can see you're on their wheel. Get within about 2.6 m and they move aside a little so you can come past.

## Privacy

- Turn off **Ride together** in ⚙ Settings to ride alone. Then nobody sees you, and you don't see them.
- Only your short name, stage, position, speed, power and cadence are shared, and only while you're riding. Closing or hiding the tab takes you off the road.
- Banned accounts never appear.
