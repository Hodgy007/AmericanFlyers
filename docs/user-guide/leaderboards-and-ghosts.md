# Leaderboards and ghosts

Every stage with a finish line has a leaderboard, and you can race a ghost of your best ride or of anyone's on the board.

## What counts

A ride counts when you start on the start line and ride to the finish. Rides that start partway along (with **Ride with** or a `?start=` link) don't count. Times are moving time from the start line to the finish line. Each rider's fastest ride is listed. You need to be signed in.

## The leaderboard (☰ Menu → 🏆 Leaderboard)

Opens the leaderboard for the stage you're on:

- **This week** (since Monday) or **All time**.
- The top 25, plus your own position if you're further down. Your row is highlighted.
- Each rider's time, the gap to the leader, and their average power.
- **Race**: puts that ride on the road as a ghost and starts you on the start line.

Endless stages such as Heartland Rollers have no finish line, so no leaderboard.

**What counts.** Only whole-stage rides from the start line, ridden at normal speed. Rides that used the 🎮 demo slider or a sped-up clock (`?timescale`) don't get a time, and the finish card says so. To keep the boards fair, times that are faster than is physically believable for the stage, rides averaging over 2000 W, and rides with no power data that are suspiciously quick are left off.

## KOM segments

A stage's **big climbs** are KOM segments: a climb of about a kilometre or more with at least 60 m of climbing (a categorised summit counts a little lower), timed from its first metre to its top, on any ride. Alpe d'Huez, Mont Ventoux, the Tourmalet, Mount Washington and Flagstaff are one segment each; Stelvio, Zoncolan, Mount Diablo, Mount Blue Sky and the Hell of the West stages have several; Morgul-Bismark's Wall is timed on each lap; Heartland Rollers and Route 66 have none.

- **In the ride.** Within 2 km of a segment a **KOM panel** appears under the climb card: its name, length and average grade, the top 5 on the board (the KOM is marked 👑) and your own rank and best. On the climb it adds a big live gap against **your best** (green and negative when you are ahead, red and positive when you are behind, for example `−0:07` or `+0:12`) and a smaller one against **your last attempt**. The gap updates once a second. On your first attempt it shows the elapsed time and *First attempt: set your time*. A small ghost of your best attempt rides ahead of or behind you (if **Race my best ride** is on and there is no whole-stage ghost).
- **At the top.** A short banner gives the result: *Alpe d'Huez — 52:14 · New PB by 0:41 · #3 of 27*, or *0:12 off your best*.
- **Hide it.** **KOM panel** in ⚙ Settings turns the panel off. Your times are still recorded.
- **What counts.** All of the segment, ridden without stopping or jumping along the road (stopping, opening the hub or ending the ride voids the effort), in moving time. Rides that used the 🎮 demo slider or a sped-up clock don't count on the boards, and aren't kept: the banner says *Demo ride, not saved*. Times faster than is physically believable are left off the shared board.
- **Where to see them.** On a route card in the hub, **⛰ KOM ×n** opens its segments: who holds the KOM, and your best. Tap one for its full board (top 10 and you, **This month** or **All time**).
- **Uploaded routes** have segments too, but only for your own times on this device: there is no shared board for them.
- **Signed out or offline** the panel shows your own best and *Sign in for the leaderboard*. Your best and last attempt are kept in this browser, and in your account when you are signed in, so the live gap follows you to another device.

## Ghosts

A ghost is a see-through blue rider replaying a past ride second by second. It moves only while you're moving, so stopping for a drink doesn't cost you.

- **Your best**: on a stage you've finished, the ghost of your fastest ride joins you automatically. Turn this off with **Race my best ride** in ⚙ Settings.
- **Anyone's ride**: press **Race** on any leaderboard row.
- The status line at the bottom shows the gap, for example *35 m behind your best*.
- To ride without a ghost, open 🏆 and choose **Ride without a ghost**.

At the finish the summary says how far ahead or behind the ghost you were, *New personal best!* if you beat your own, and then where you placed on the leaderboard.

Only the distance trace of a ride is shared for its ghost. Power, heart rate and other details stay private.
