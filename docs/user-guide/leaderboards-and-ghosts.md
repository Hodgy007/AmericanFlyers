# Leaderboards and ghosts

Every stage with a finish line has a leaderboard, and you can race a ghost of your best ride or of anyone's on the board.

## What counts

A ride counts when you start on the start line and ride to the finish. Rides that start partway along (with **Ride with** or a `?start=` link) don't count. Times are moving time from the start line to the finish line. Each rider's fastest ride is listed. You need to be signed in.

## The leaderboard (☰ Menu → 🏆 Leaderboard)

Opens the leaderboard for the stage you're on:

- **This week** (since Monday) or **All time**.
- The top 25, plus your own position if you're further down. Your row is highlighted.
- Each rider's time, the gap to the leader, and their average power.
- **Race**: puts that ride on the road as a ghost and starts you on the start line.

Endless stages such as Heartland Rollers have no finish line, so no leaderboard.

**What counts.** Only whole-stage rides from the start line, ridden at normal speed. Rides that used the 🎮 demo slider or a sped-up clock (`?timescale`) don't get a time, and the finish card says so. To keep the boards fair, times that are faster than is physically believable for the stage, rides averaging over 2000 W, and rides with no power data that are suspiciously quick are left off.

## Ghosts

A ghost is a see-through blue rider replaying a past ride second by second. It moves only while you're moving, so stopping for a drink doesn't cost you.

- **Your best**: on a stage you've finished, the ghost of your fastest ride joins you automatically. Turn this off with **Race my best ride** in ⚙ Settings.
- **Anyone's ride**: press **Race** on any leaderboard row.
- The status line at the bottom shows the gap, for example *35 m behind your best*.
- To ride without a ghost, open 🏆 and choose **Ride without a ghost**.

At the finish the summary says how far ahead or behind the ghost you were, *New personal best!* if you beat your own, and then where you placed on the leaderboard.

Only the distance trace of a ride is shared for its ghost. Power, heart rate and other details stay private.
