# Training plans

A training plan is a few weeks of sessions. Each session is a workout or a stage to ride. Open **🏋 Workouts → 📅 Training plans**.

## The plans

| Plan | Level | Length | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Get Started | Beginner | 3 weeks, 3 rides a week | Learn the app and your numbers, finishing on Alpe d'Huez. |
| FTP Builder | Intermediate | 6 weeks, 3–4 rides a week | Sweet spot and threshold work, an easy week, and a retest. |
| Hell of the West | All levels | 4 weeks, 3–4 rides a week | Tempo, climbing and race efforts, building to the final stage from *American Flyers*. |

## Following a plan

- **Start plan** begins the plan. The next session appears at the top with a **Ride this workout** or **Ride this stage** button.
- Sessions tick themselves off: a workout when you finish it, a stage when you reach the finish line. Heartland Rollers has no finish, so 30 minutes of riding counts.
- Tick or untick any session by hand with its round button.
- **Leave this plan** clears your progress.

Your plan and progress are saved in your settings. When you're signed in, they follow you to other devices.

## Workouts added for the plans

Recovery Spin 30, Tempo 2×20, Threshold 2×15, Endurance 75 and Openers 30 are now built in. You can ride them on their own from the workout library, too.
