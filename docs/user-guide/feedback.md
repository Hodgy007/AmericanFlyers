# Feedback

This page explains how to send an idea or a bug report, what the app attaches to it, the status labels riders see, and how replies work.

## Send feedback

1. Sign in. Feedback needs an account, so that replies can reach you.
2. Click **💬** in the top bar.
3. Choose a type:
   - **💡 Idea or improvement**
   - **🐞 Something's broken**
   - **💬 Other**
4. Write your message. It needs at least three characters, and can be up to 4,000.
5. Click **Send**.

The app adds some details to your message automatically: the stage you're on, the workout (if any), your trainer's name or *demo*, your browser, your screen size, and the app's version. This helps when chasing a bug. Don't put personal information in the message itself.

## Your suggestions

Below the form is **Your suggestions**: everything you've sent, newest first. Each item shows its type, its status and the date. If an admin has replied, the reply appears under your message.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Received, not yet reviewed. |
| **Planned** | Accepted and planned. |
| **In progress** | Being worked on. |
| **Done** | Finished. |
| **Declined** | Won't be done. |

Admins set the status and write the reply. See [Admin dashboard](../admin-guide/admin-dashboard.md#feedback-inbox).

## Editing and deleting

Once sent, you can't edit or delete feedback. To change what you said, send a new message.
