# AI coach and AI rivals

Two optional features that use AI (Claude, made by Anthropic): **Coach Marty**, who gives tips as you ride and a debrief when you finish, and four **rivals** who ride with you, talk, and remember your rides together. Both are **off until you turn them on**, and you need an account.

## Turning them on

1. Open the app signed in (you always are when you ride).
2. ⚙ Settings → **AI coach and rivals**. Tick **AI coach**, **AI rivals**, or both, then **Save**.
3. Optionally tick **Speak their lines aloud** to hear the coach and rivals with your browser's voice.

What is sent when they are on: your first name, FTP and weight, the numbers from the ride (power, heart rate, cadence, distance, climbing, time in zones, the stage and your race place), and your last four weeks of totals. Never your email address. Untick a switch to stop it. See [Privacy and your data](privacy-and-your-data.md).

The rides never wait for the AI. If it is busy, offline, or you have used your messages for the day, you get built-in tips and lines instead.

## Coach Marty

Coach Marty is a gruff-but-warm former team mechanic, an AI character.

- **Ride plan.** A few seconds into a ride he plans it from the stage's climbs, sprints and summits (or from a workout's steps) and your FTP. Each tip appears in a card at the bottom left for 6 seconds when you reach that spot, up to 8 per ride.
- **Reactive tips.** At most one every 5 minutes, when you: spin under 60 rpm on a climb for 30 seconds; see your heart rate drift up by more than 8% at steady power; go 20% over the target in an ERG workout; or ride within 5 m of a rider without being in their draft.
- **Debrief.** When you finish a stage or workout, or press Reset after 3 minutes or more, the summary shows a headline, a short summary, two things that went well, two to work on, and a suggested next session from the built-in workouts and stages, with a **Load it** button. Debriefs are kept with the ride and show again in your profile.

Daily limits (UTC day): 10 debriefs, 6 plans and 30 tips.

## Rivals

☰ Menu → **🤺 Rivals** shows four original rivals:

| Rival | Rides like |
| --- | --- |
| 🔥 **Kit Blaze** | A showboat sprinter. Smooth until the last 300 m, then flat out. |
| ❄️ **Sasha Frost** | A cold, calm climber. Attacks when the road passes 6%. |
| 🦊 **Gus Fenwick** | A crafty veteran. Sits on your wheel and pounces when you ease off. |
| 📈 **Tess Wattson** | A data-obsessed time triallist. One steady power; surges don't bother her. |

Pick one and **Ride with** them: they ride at about your recent pace (with a little rubber-banding so it stays close) and attack in their own style. Their name tag and a speech bubble float above them, and what they say also shows as a toast. They react when you pass them, when they attack or drop you, when you drop them, in the last kilometre and at the finish.

- **Memory and record.** With **AI rivals** on, the dialog shows your head-to-head record and what each rival remembers ("last time you cracked on the Wall..."), and they use it in their lines. **Forget me** wipes one rival's memory of you.
- **Without AI**, rivals still ride and talk with ready-made lines. They just do not write fresh lines or remember you.
- **Demo rides and sped-up (`?timescale`) rides do not count** in your record, though the rival still says goodbye.

Daily limits (UTC day): 10 sets of opening lines and 10 closing lines.

## If something goes wrong

- *"Turn on the AI coach or AI rivals in Settings first"*: the switch is off for your account.
- *Used all of today's messages*: they come back at midnight UTC; built-in tips and lines carry on.
- No AI at all: the site owner may not have set it up yet. Everything else works.
