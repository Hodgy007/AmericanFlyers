# Racing and the Hell of the West tour

This page covers racing the pro teams over a stage, the live race panel, the classifications and the three-stage **Hell of the West tour** from the film *American Flyers*.

## Racing a stage

Any stage with a finish line can be raced. Open **🏁 Stage** and press **🏁 Race this stage** on its card. (Free riding is unchanged: choosing a stage by clicking its card still gives you the steady pro teams.)

1. You roll up to the line with the whole field: 12 pro teams of three riders, 36 riders in all, lined up just behind you.
2. A **3, 2, 1** countdown beeps and the race starts at **GO!**. You can't move until then.
3. The field races with you. They are the same computer-ridden teams as on the road (see [Riding together](riding-together.md#pro-teams)), but now they act like a race:
   - **Attacks.** On the climbs, strong riders surge at 120 to 150% of their normal power for 30 to 90 seconds, sometimes with a team-mate.
   - **Breakaways.** Early in a long stage, two to four riders from different teams get away.
   - **Chases.** When a group is getting away, the bunch raises its pace to bring it back, and winds it up in the last 15% of the stage.
   - **Cracks.** On climbs, riders who can't hold the pace drop off the back and form their own groups. Groups merge when they catch each other.
   - **Sprints.** In the last 400 m, and before each sprint banner, riders open up a short, huge sprint.
4. **Draft.** You can sit in any group's slipstream as usual; the draft gauge works in a race.

The riders are scaled to **you**: the field's strength follows your FTP and weight (set them in ⚙ Settings), so the race is close whatever your level. Riding at about your FTP keeps you with the middle of the field.

Races at set times, with other riders, are in **☰ Menu → 🗓 Events**: see [Group rides, races and the commentator](events-and-commentary.md). A commentator calls every race; turn him off in Settings.

## The race panel

A small panel on the left (on the right if your data blocks are on the left) shows:

- your **position** out of 37, the race clock and your gap to the front of the race;
- the **groups on the road**: the break, the bunch and so on, with their size and gap, and the one you are in highlighted;
- the next **sprint banner** or **summit** and how far away it is;
- the holders of the **yellow** (leader), **green** (points) and **polka-dot** (mountains) jerseys right now;
- a line for what just happened, such as *Hugo and Matteo attack!*.

## Points, summits and jerseys

- **Sprint points** go to the first riders over each intermediate sprint banner (8, 5, 3, 2, 1) and over the finish line (20, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 3, 2, 1).
- **Mountain points** go to the first riders over each climb's summit. Harder climbs pay more: a category 4 climb gives 1 point to the first rider, a category 3 gives 3, 2, 1, a category 2 gives 6, 4, 2, 1, a category 1 gives 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1, and an hors catégorie climb gives 20, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2. Summits are found from each stage's profile, or named by the stage.
- **Time**: your stage time is the race clock when you cross the line (not your moving time). Riders finishing within a second of the rider ahead get the same time.
- **Jerseys**: yellow for the lowest total time, green for the most sprint points, polka dots for the most mountain points.

## The results

When you cross the line, the finish card lists the **top 20 and you**, with tabs for the stage result, the points and the mountains. In the tour there is also the **General** classification (total time). The leaders carry jersey dots. Press **Race again**, or **Keep riding**.

Your ride still counts for your own records: it is saved to your account, and a whole-stage ride goes on the stage's leaderboard with your moving time. There is no ghost in a race.

## The Hell of the West tour

Open **☰ Menu → 🏆 Hell of the West tour** (or the tour card at the top of the stage picker) and press **Start the tour**. You race the three stages of the film in order against the same riders:

| Stage | Where | Distance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Morgul-Bismark** | The circuit south of Boulder, Colorado, up The Wall (18%) at the end of each lap | 2 laps, 42.3 km |
| 2. **Tour of the Moon** | Rim Rock Drive, Colorado National Monument: switchbacks, tunnels, red rock canyons, up to 2,024 m | 37 km |
| 3. **Echo Lake** | The existing Hell of the West: Idaho Springs up Chicago Creek to the sprint at Echo Lake | 20.8 km |

After each stage you see the stage result and the general, points and mountains classifications, with the yellow, green and polka-dot leaders. The tour dialog keeps your progress, and it syncs with your account.

- **Simulate it.** Short of time? **Simulate it** runs the next stage in a moment, with you riding at about 95% of your FTP. The whole field is simulated, so you still get a result and the classifications.
- **Restart the tour** throws the tour away and starts again with a new field. Pressing **Reset** during a stage ends that race; the tour stays on that stage until you race it again.
- Racing a tour stage from the stage picker (**Race this stage**) counts for the tour if a tour is under way; racing a stage again replaces its result.

The tour stages are also ordinary stages: ride them freely, race them on their own, and find their leaderboards like any other. The third stage keeps its original name, **Hell of the West**, and its leaderboard.

## Limits

- Races start from the start line, so `?start=` isn't used in a race.
- The route's bends and rolling hills between the published landmarks are approximated. See [Stages and routes](stages-and-routes.md).
- The field's riders don't change kit for the jerseys, and your own kit doesn't change either yet.
