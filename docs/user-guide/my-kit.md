# My kit

Design your own jersey and bike. Your rider wears it on your screen, and other riders on your stage see it too.

Open **☰ Menu → 🎨 My kit**.

## Choosing colours

- **Jersey**: the colour of your jersey.
- **Second colour**: your shorts and trim.
- **Bike frame**: the colour of your frame.

Click a colour swatch to pick a new one. The little drawing at the top shows your jersey and bike, and your rider in the world behind updates as you choose.

## Presets

Tap a preset to start from a ready-made kit:

- **American Flyers**: the original stars and stripes. This is the default.
- **Sunrise Social**, **Forest Rollers**, **Storm Chasers**, **Berry Blast**, **Night Ride** and **Lemon Zest**: made-up club kits.

You can pick a preset and then change any of its colours.

## Saving

Press **Save kit** to keep it. **Cancel** (or ✕) puts your rider back as it was. Your kit is saved with your settings, so when you're signed in it follows you to other browsers.

## What other riders see

When you ride together with other signed-in riders, your kit is sent along with your position, and they see your jersey and bike colours on the road, and their name tag for you gets a stripe in your jersey colour. You see theirs in the same way. Riders who haven't chosen a kit appear in a colour picked for them.

Only the three colours are shared. Switch off **Ride together** in ⚙ Settings and nobody sees your kit.
