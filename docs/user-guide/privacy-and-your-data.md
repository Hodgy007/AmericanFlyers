# Privacy and your data

The Privacy page (☰ Menu → 🔒 Privacy) explains in plain English what American Flyers collects, what other riders see, where it is kept and for how long. This page covers what you can do with your data.

## What other riders see

Your first name and last initial, your position, speed and kit while you ride on the same stage, and your finish times on leaderboards. Never your email, weight, FTP or heart rate.

## Download my data

Profile → **🔒 Privacy & data** → **Download my data** saves one JSON file with your account details, settings, rides (including the second-by-second readings), workouts, routes, FTP history, feedback, and the ride-ons you've sent and received.

## Delete my account

Profile → **🔒 Privacy & data** → type `DELETE` → **Delete my account for good**. This permanently removes your account and everything in it, signs you out, and clears what American Flyers saved in this browser. It can't be undone, so download your data first if you want a copy.

Admin accounts can't be deleted from the app; the message tells you what to do (ask another admin to remove you from the admin list first).

## Riding without an account

Everything stays in your own browser. There is nothing on our side to delete.
