# Installing the app, first-ride setup and big screens

## First-ride setup

The first time you ride, a three-step guide opens:

1. **About you**: units, weight, FTP and max heart rate. *I don't know mine* fills in a starting FTP from your weight. Entering your age fills in an estimated max heart rate.
2. **Your trainer**: connect your smart trainer and heart-rate strap.
3. **Your first ride**: the Ramp FTP Test, the Hell of the West stage, a training plan, a free ride or a demo ride.

**Skip setup** or Escape closes it. Run it again from ⚙ Settings → *Run the setup guide again*. Signing in on a new device with an account that already has settings skips it.

## Install as an app

American Flyers can be installed like an app. It then opens full screen, with its own icon.

- **Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Android**: press **⬇ Install app** in the top bar (it appears when the browser offers it), or use the browser's *Install* menu item.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari)**: Share → *Add to Home Screen*. Safari can't use Bluetooth, so use Bluefy to connect a trainer.

Once installed, the app opens quickly, and the 3D models and textures load from the device after the first ride.

## Full screen and TVs

- **⛶** or the **F** key toggles full screen.
- On big screens (1800 px wide and up, such as a TV across the room) the numbers, workout panel and gear panel are drawn larger, and larger again from 2600 px.
