# Garage, credits and challenges

Ride, earn credits, level up, and spend them on better bikes, wheels and clothes. Open **☰ Menu → 🚲 Garage & store**.

## Levels and credits

Credits and levels come from your **saved rides**, counted by the server, so they can't be faked.

| You ride | You earn |
| --- | --- |
| Each km | 1 credit and 5 XP |
| Each 10 m climbed | 1 credit (0.5 XP per metre) |
| A weekly challenge you claim | its credits, and 25 XP |

XP gives you a level from 1 to 50 on a gentle curve (level 2 at 100 XP, level 10 at about 3,400 XP, level 50 at about 50,000 XP), with a title every five levels. Demo rides, rides without power data and rides run on a sped-up clock don't count.

Sign in to earn: signed-out riders can browse the store and see what they would earn.

## Bikes, wheels, tyres and clothing

Tap an item to see it on the turntable (drag to turn it). Each shows its price, what unlocks it, and **what it does in plain numbers**, worked out from the same physics as the ride: "Saves ≈ 7 W at 40 km/h" and "≈ 25 s faster up Alpe d'Huez" for a rider like you.

| Item | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Aero road frame | 2.5% less drag |
| Climbing frame | 0.8 kg lighter |
| Time-trial bike | 5% less drag, 0.4 kg heavier; tri-bars; unlocks disc wheels |
| The Tron bike | 2.5% less drag, 0.3 kg lighter |
| 50 mm carbon wheels | 1.5% less drag |
| 80 mm deep wheels | 2.5% less drag, 0.25 kg heavier |
| Lightweight climbing wheels | 0.45 kg lighter |
| Disc wheel (time-trial bike only) | 3.5% less drag, 0.4 kg heavier |
| Race tyres | a little less rolling resistance |
| Aero helmet | 1.5% less drag |
| Skinsuit | 2% less drag |

Jersey designs (stripes, hoops, chevrons, stars, polka dots), black helmets and coloured socks are looks only. Designs use the jersey and second colours you chose in [My kit](my-kit.md).

Press **Equip** to wear something you own. **Buy** spends credits (and needs a level). Other riders see your bike and clothes.

## The Tron bike

Climb **8,848 m in total** (the height of Everest) across your saved rides and the Tron bike unlocks by itself: a dark frame with neon light-ribbon edges, light-ribbon wheels and a pool of light on the road. It pulses gently and glows brighter at night. Once unlocked it stays yours, even if you delete rides.

## This week's challenges

The **This week** tab shows four challenges that change every Monday (UTC): a climbing one, a distance one, a stages one and a special one (win a race, finish on a podium, ride with a pro team for 20 km, or ride on four days). Finish one and press **Claim** for its credits, once.

## Share your ride

When you finish a stage, or open a ride in your profile, press **📤 Share** for a 1080 × 1350 picture with your route, time, distance, climbing, power, place, badges and your kit colours. On a phone it opens the share sheet; on a computer it saves a PNG.
