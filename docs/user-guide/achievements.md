# Achievements

This page lists every achievement, its icon, and how to earn it. Achievements are worked out from your saved rides and FTP history, so they appear in your profile's **Achievements** tab as you ride.

## How achievements are shown

The **Achievements** tab shows a card for each achievement, with its icon, name, description, a progress bar and a detail line. Earned achievements show a check mark and the date you earned them. Achievements counted over time (such as total distance) show how far you are toward the goal.

Achievements aren't stored separately. They're recalculated from your rides, so an achievement can appear as soon as the ride that earns it is saved.

## The achievements

| Icon | Name | How to earn it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🚴 | First pedal strokes | Save your first ride. |
| ⏱ | Hour of power | Ride for at least an hour in one go. |
| 🏁 | Route 66 time-trial | Finish the Route 66 Flats stage. |
| ⛰ | Alpe d'Huez | Finish the Alpe d'Huez stage. |
| 🏔 | Stelvio | Finish the Stelvio Pass stage. |
| 🧪 | Tested | Complete a ramp FTP test. |
| ⚡ | Sprinter | Hold 800 W for 5 seconds. |
| 🔥 | Four watts | Hold 4 W/kg for 20 minutes. The target uses your current rider weight. |
| 💯 | Century | Ride 100 km in one ride. |
| 🛣 | Coast to coast | Ride 500 km in total. |
| 🌎 | Round the block | Ride 2,000 km in total. |
| 🧗 | Everesting | Climb 8,849 m in total, the height of Everest. |
| 🕰 | Saddle time | Ride for 24 hours in total. |
| 🏋 | Workout regular | Complete 10 workouts. |
| 📆 | Three in a row | Ride on three days in a row. |
| 🗓 | Consistency | Ride in each of four weeks in a row. Weeks start on Monday. |
| 📈 | Getting stronger | Raise your FTP by 10 W from your first recorded value. |

## Notes

- **Stage achievements** need the stage to be finished, not just ridden.
- **Tested** matches the workout name *Ramp FTP Test*, so it counts the built-in ramp test.
- **Workout regular** counts workouts you completed, not separate workouts. Riding the same one ten times counts ten times.
- **Four watts** uses your current rider weight in Settings. Changing your weight changes the target.
- **Getting stronger** needs at least two FTP entries in your history. See [Zones and FTP](zones-and-ftp.md#ftp-history).
- Totals use your most recent 1,000 rides. Rides beyond that aren't counted. See [Accounts and profile](accounts-and-profile.md).
