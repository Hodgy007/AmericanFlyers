# The hub

This page covers the hub, the screen you see before you ride: what is on it, how to choose what to ride, and how to get back to it.

## When you see it

The hub comes first, every time:

- **Signed in.** Opening the app takes you straight to your hub. There's no home page to click through.
- **Not signed in.** On the home page, sign in (Google, or email and password) or choose **Ride without an account**. Either way you arrive at the hub. As a guest the sections that need an account show a friendly sign-in prompt instead of your numbers.
- **After a ride.** Open **☰ Menu → 🏠 Hub** or press **🏠 Back to the hub** on a finish or summary card.

Behind the hub the live 3D world turns slowly round your rider, blurred. On phones, or if your device is set to reduce motion, it barely moves to save battery.

## The header

- **You.** Your name, your level and its title, an XP bar towards the next level, and your credits (see [Garage, credits and challenges](garage-and-credits.md)). Your totals (distance, climbing, rides) sit underneath.
- **Up next.** What the big red **Ride** button will start, with the route's elevation profile or the workout's blocks. Press it, or press **Enter** (it has the keyboard focus when the hub opens).
- **Free ride.** Starts Heartland Rollers, the endless road, with no workout.
- **Your plan.** If you follow a [training plan](training-plans.md), its next session shows here with **Ride it**.

On a phone, a small bar at the bottom keeps **Ride** in reach once the header has scrolled away.

## Choosing what to ride

Pick a card in **Route** or **Training** and **Up next** changes. Nothing starts until you press **Ride**.

- Picking a **route** rides that route, with no workout.
- Picking a **workout** rides it on a road with no finish line: your chosen route if it has none, otherwise Heartland Rollers. **Up next** says which. Use **✕ Clear workout** to go back to just the route.

The app remembers the last route you started from the hub. A route in the address bar (`?stage=alpe-dhuez`) takes priority.

## The sections

On a computer the sections are tabs (use the **←** and **→** keys on the tab row). On a phone they're one column you scroll, with the same tabs along the top as links.

| Section | What it shows | What it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **🏁 Route** | Every stage and climb, plus your uploaded GPX routes, each with its elevation profile, length, climbing and your best time. **🏁 Race** starts a race against the pro teams. | The Hell of the West tour, the stage picker (to delete uploaded routes) and the GPX upload. |
| **🏋 Training** | The Ramp FTP test first, then your own workouts, then the built-in ones, with their block charts. | **＋ Create a workout** and the workout library. |
| **📅 Plan** | The plan you follow: progress, a dot for each session, the next session with **Ride it**, and what comes after. Without a plan, the three plans with **Start this plan**. | The full plan. |
| **🏅 Achievements** | The badges you've earned and the next few to go, with progress. | The profile's Achievements tab. |
| **🚲 Garage** | Your bike and kit on a turntable (drag to turn it), level and credits, what you're wearing, and this week's challenges with progress. | The garage and store, the weekly challenges and *My kit*. |
| **📊 Stats** | This week and the last four weeks (distance, time, climbing, TSS, rides), your FTP and its history, distance for the last eight weeks, and your last five rides. | A ride's full detail in your profile, and the profile's Stats tab. |
| **🗓 Events** | The next race and the next group ride, with a live countdown and **Join**. | The Events list. |

Details open in the dialogs you already know, over the hub. Close one (or press **Escape**) and you're back where you were.

The turntable and the charts load only when you look at them, and your numbers fill in as your account answers. The hub remembers your last visit, so it appears at once.

## Going back to the hub

**☰ Menu → 🏠 Hub** (or the **★ American Flyers** name in the top bar, when signed in) ends the ride and shows the hub.

If you've ridden a minute or more, or you're in a race or group ride, you're asked first:

- **Keep riding** closes the question and nothing changes.
- **Save and go to the hub** saves the ride to your account (as **End / reset ride** does) and shows the hub. If you're not signed in the button says **End ride and go to the hub**, because a guest's ride can't be saved.

A ride that's already finished needs no question, and neither does one under a minute.

## Keyboard

- **Tab** moves through the buttons and cards in order; **Enter** or **Space** chooses a card.
- **← / →**, **Home** and **End** move between the section tabs.
- **Escape** closes a dialog, or with none open brings you back to **Ride**. It never leaves you stuck.
- The ride's own shortcuts (**C**, **M**, **F**, the arrow keys) are off while the hub is showing.

## If something is missing

- *My name and level show as grey bars.* Your account is still answering. They appear in a moment.
- *There are no best times or stats.* Both come from rides saved to your account. See [Accounts and profile](accounts-and-profile.md).
- *The turntable is blank.* Your browser couldn't make a second 3D view. Use **Open the garage** instead.
