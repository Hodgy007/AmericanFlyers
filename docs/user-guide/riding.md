# Riding

This page is a tour of the riding screen: the top bar, the data blocks, the status line and elevation strip, the camera views, demo controls, and how Reset and the ride summary work.

## The top bar

Buttons across the top of the screen, from left to right:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **🏁 Stage** | Opens the stage picker. See [Stages and routes](stages-and-routes.md). |
| **🏋 Workouts** | Opens the workout library and training plans. See [Workouts](workouts.md) and [Training plans](training-plans.md). |
| **👥** | Who else is riding this stage. See [Riding together](riding-together.md). |
| **Connect trainer** / **Disconnect** | Connects or disconnects your trainer. See [Connecting devices](connecting-devices.md). |
| **☰ Menu** | Everything else, listed below. Click outside it or press Escape to close it. |
| **👤 Sign in** (or your first name) | Opens sign-in, or your profile when you're signed in. |

## Steering

You ride on the right half of the road and can move across it. Press **←** or **→** to move about 0.7 m (hold to keep going); on a touch screen use the **◀ ▶** buttons in the bottom corners. The bike eases across in under a second and the camera follows. **↑** and **↓** still change the demo power.

Why steer? To line up on someone's wheel for the draft (see [Riding together](riding-together.md#drafting)), or to pass them cleanly. With **Auto-steer into the draft** on in ⚙ Settings (the default), if you haven't steered for about 3 seconds and a rider is within 12 m ahead, you drift onto their line by yourself. Steering yourself pauses it for 3 seconds.

## Time of day and weather

☰ Menu → **🌤 Time & weather** sets the light (Auto, Dawn, Day, Dusk, Night) and the weather (Auto, Clear, Cloudy, Rain). It applies straight away and is saved with your settings.

- **Auto** time starts at the stage's usual time (Route 66 and Hell of the West start at dusk, the others in daylight) and moves on as you ride, about an hour of light every two minutes, so a long ride can run from dusk into night and on to dawn.
- **Auto** weather uses the stage's own (the Tourmalet is cloudy, the rest clear) and now and then changes.
- **Night** has a moon and stars and a soft light from your front light on the road ahead. **Rain** brings streaks, thicker mist, a darker, glossier road and a gentle rain sound (with sound on).

## Wind and echelons

There's a breeze on every stage, and it changes with the weather: light on a clear day, stronger under cloud, strongest in the rain, with gusts. The **wind chip** at the right of the status line shows an arrow (up is the way you ride, so an arrow pointing down is a headwind), the speed, and words such as *Headwind*, *Tailwind* or *Crosswind from the left*.

- A **headwind** slows you for the same power; a **tailwind** helps. Your smart trainer is told about the headwind too, so it feels harder or easier.
- In a **crosswind** the shelter behind a rider isn't straight behind them but on their downwind side: about a quarter of a metre sideways for every metre per second of crosswind, up to about 0.8 m. Line up diagonally and you keep the draft. The draft gauge's *Move ◀ / ▶* hint points at the sheltered spot, and auto-steer aims for it.
- The pro teams ride a diagonal **echelon** when it blows across the road, in a race as well as on a free ride.
- Clouds drift with the wind, and the rain slants.

Switch the wind off with **Wind** in ⚙ Settings if you'd rather ride in still air.

## Mini-map and climb preview

A round **mini-map** in the corner shows the shape of the route with you (white dot, red nose), other riders (blue), the pro teams (their colours), your ghost (a ring) and the finish (checkered). In a race it also marks intermediate sprints (green diamonds) and climb summits (red triangles). Click it to switch between the whole route with north up and a view that follows your heading and shows the road ahead. Turn it on or off in **☰ Menu → 🗺 Mini-map** or in ⚙ Settings.

When a **climb** (steeper than 3% for more than 500 m) starts within 2 km, a card appears under the data blocks: *Next climb in 1.4 km, 3.2 km at 7.1%, max 11%*, with a coloured profile. Once you're on it the card shows the distance to the top and the average grade still to come. The card is part of the same setting.

## Data blocks

The row of numbers across the top is made of **data blocks**. The first block is the largest. The **✎** button at the right of the row opens the chooser, where you pick which of the 24 fields to show and in what order. See [Data blocks](data-blocks.md).

## The status line

The bottom-left corner shows two things:

- **Trainer status**, with a colored dot: connected, searching, or not connected.
- **Stage name and progress.** On a stage with a finish line, this shows the distance to go and the next numbered hairpin, for example *Alpe d'Huez · 8.4 km to go · next: Virage 14 in 230 m*. On Heartland Rollers it shows just the name.

## Panels at the bottom of the screen

- **Draft gauge**: appears when another rider is within 60 m ahead. It shows the gap to their back wheel and, once you're within 12 m, how much drag you're saving. If you're close but off to one side, it tells you which way to move (*Move ◀ into their wheel*). See [Riding together](riding-together.md#drafting).
- **Steering buttons (◀ ▶)**: on touch screens, in the bottom corners. See [Steering](#steering).
- **Gear panel**: with virtual shifting on, your gear and − / + buttons. See [Sound and virtual shifting](sound-and-shifting.md).
- **Demo power**: in a demo ride, the power slider.

## The elevation strip

The bottom-right strip shows the road ahead, colored by grade:

- **Blue:** descending.
- **Green:** flat or gentle.
- **Yellow:** moderate.
- **Orange:** steep.
- **Red:** very steep (8% or more).

On an endless stage it shows about 0.5 km behind you and 4.5 km ahead. On a stage with a finish line it shows the whole stage, with your position marked.

During a workout the strip shows the workout's graph instead, with your power trace drawn over it. See [Workouts](workouts.md).

## Camera views

Press **C**, or click the camera button, to cycle through:

1. **Chase view:** behind and above the rider. This is the default.
2. **Side view:** beside the bike.
3. **Rider's view:** from the rider's eyes. The rider model is hidden.

## Demo controls

When the demo ride is on, a panel shows **Demo power** with a slider from 0 to 600 W. Use the slider, or the **↑** and **↓** keys, which change power by 10 W. In a workout, the demo rider follows the workout's target.

## Reset and the ride summary

Press **Reset** to finish the ride:

- **Under one minute:** the ride is cleared straight away. Nothing is saved.
- **One minute or more:** a **Ride summary** appears with the time, distance, average power, normalized power, average heart rate, climbing and the heart-rate and power zones.

The summary has two buttons:

- **Keep riding** closes the summary and keeps your ride going.
- **Start a new ride** clears the ride and starts again.

If you're signed in, the ride is saved when the summary appears. If you're not, the summary offers a **Sign in** link to keep the ride. See [Accounts and profile](accounts-and-profile.md#when-rides-are-saved).

## Stage finish screen

On a stage with a finish line, crossing the line ends the ride with a **finished** screen: time, average power, average speed, climbing, average heart rate and normalized power, plus the zones. The buttons are **Keep riding** and **Ride it again**.

Choosing a different stage also resets the ride. See [Stages and routes](stages-and-routes.md).
