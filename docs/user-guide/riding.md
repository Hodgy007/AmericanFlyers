# Riding

This page is a tour of the riding screen: the top bar, the data blocks, the status line and elevation strip, the camera views, demo controls, and how Reset and the ride summary work.

## The top bar

Buttons across the top of the screen, from left to right:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **🏁 Stage** | Opens the stage picker. See [Stages and routes](stages-and-routes.md). |
| **🏋 Workouts** | Opens the workout library and training plans. See [Workouts](workouts.md) and [Training plans](training-plans.md). |
| **👥** | Who else is riding this stage. See [Riding together](riding-together.md). |
| **Connect trainer** / **Disconnect** | Connects or disconnects your trainer. See [Connecting devices](connecting-devices.md). |
| **☰ Menu** | Everything else, listed below. Click outside it or press Escape to close it. |
| **👤 Sign in** (or your first name) | Opens sign-in, or your profile when you're signed in. |

The **☰ Menu** holds:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **🎮 Demo ride** / **Stop demo** | Turns the demo ride on or off. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#try-the-demo-ride). |
| **❤️ Heart rate strap** / **Heart rate ✓** | Connects or disconnects a heart-rate strap. |
| **🎥 Chase view** (or Side view, Rider's view) | Changes the camera. The label shows the current view. Shortcut: **C**. |
| **🏆 Leaderboard** | The leaderboard for this stage. See [Leaderboards and ghosts](leaderboards-and-ghosts.md). |
| **📅 Training plans** | See [Training plans](training-plans.md). |
| **🔊 Sound on** / **🔇 Sound off** | Shortcut: **M**. See [Sound and virtual shifting](sound-and-shifting.md). |
| **⛶ Full screen** | Shortcut: **F**. |
| **⬇ Install app** | Shown when your browser can install the app. See [Installing the app](installing-the-app.md). |
| **↺ End / reset ride** | Ends the ride and shows its summary. See below. |
| **⚙ Settings** | Opens Settings. |
| **💬 Ideas & bug reports** | Requires sign-in. |
| **🛠 Admin dashboard** | Only shown to admins. |

On narrow screens the buttons wrap onto more rows.

## Data blocks

The row of numbers across the top is made of **data blocks**. The first block is the largest. The **✎** button at the right of the row opens the chooser, where you pick which of the 24 fields to show and in what order. See [Data blocks](data-blocks.md).

## The status line

The bottom-left corner shows two things:

- **Trainer status**, with a colored dot: connected, searching, or not connected.
- **Stage name and progress.** On a stage with a finish line, this shows the distance to go and the next numbered hairpin, for example *Alpe d'Huez · 8.4 km to go · next: Virage 14 in 230 m*. On Heartland Rollers it shows just the name.

## The elevation strip

The bottom-right strip shows the road ahead, colored by grade:

- **Blue:** descending.
- **Green:** flat or gentle.
- **Yellow:** moderate.
- **Orange:** steep.
- **Red:** very steep (8% or more).

On an endless stage it shows about 0.5 km behind you and 4.5 km ahead. On a stage with a finish line it shows the whole stage, with your position marked.

During a workout the strip shows the workout's graph instead, with your power trace drawn over it. See [Workouts](workouts.md).

## Camera views

Press **C**, or click the camera button, to cycle through:

1. **Chase view:** behind and above the rider. This is the default.
2. **Side view:** beside the bike.
3. **Rider's view:** from the rider's eyes. The rider model is hidden.

## Demo controls

When the demo ride is on, a panel shows **Demo power** with a slider from 0 to 600 W. Use the slider, or the **↑** and **↓** keys, which change power by 10 W. In a workout, the demo rider follows the workout's target.

## Reset and the ride summary

Press **Reset** to finish the ride:

- **Under one minute:** the ride is cleared straight away. Nothing is saved.
- **One minute or more:** a **Ride summary** appears with the time, distance, average power, normalized power, average heart rate, climbing and the heart-rate and power zones.

The summary has two buttons:

- **Keep riding** closes the summary and keeps your ride going.
- **Start a new ride** clears the ride and starts again.

If you're signed in, the ride is saved when the summary appears. If you're not, the summary offers a **Sign in** link to keep the ride. See [Accounts and profile](accounts-and-profile.md#when-rides-are-saved).

## Stage finish screen

On a stage with a finish line, crossing the line ends the ride with a **finished** screen: time, average power, average speed, climbing, average heart rate and normalized power, plus the zones. The buttons are **Keep riding** and **Ride it again**.

Choosing a different stage also resets the ride. See [Stages and routes](stages-and-routes.md).
