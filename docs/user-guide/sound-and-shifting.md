# Sound and virtual shifting

## Sound

Rides have sound, made in the browser with no downloads:

- Wind and tyre hum that rise with your speed.
- The freewheel ticking when you stop pedalling.
- Crowds cheering on the hairpins of the climbs and over the last 700 m to the finish, with the odd whistle.
- Beeps at 3, 2 and 1 seconds before a workout step changes, and a long beep as it changes.
- A fanfare at the finish line.

Turn sound on or off with the 🔊 button or the **M** key. Browsers start sound only after your first click or key press.

## Virtual shifting

Turn on **Virtual shifting** in ⚙ Settings to choose your own gear, like on a real bike. Leave your trainer's real chain in one gear.

- A gear panel appears at the bottom of the screen with **−** and **+** buttons. Keys: **−** or **[** for an easier gear, **+**, **=** or **]** for a harder one.
- There are 24 gears, from 0.75 (easier than 34×34) to 4.8 (a little harder than 53×11). Gear 12 feels like the plain road.
- Harder gears make the trainer push back harder, and easier gears lighten it, on top of the road's grade. Each shift reaches the trainer at once.
- The rider's legs on screen turn at the cadence your gear gives at your speed.
- In ERG workouts the trainer holds the target power, so gears don't change the resistance.
- In a demo ride, the cadence shown follows your gear.
