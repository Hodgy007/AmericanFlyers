# Garage, credits and challenges (developer notes)

**Server is the source of truth** for credits, XP, levels, purchases and challenge claims (`db/schema.sql`, "garage, credits, levels, challenges"). The browser only displays them and applies equipment locally.

- `wallet_of(uid)` (internal) derives km and climbing from `activities` (power data present, `avg_power <= 1500`, at least 60 s, not `demo`, distance and climb capped by duration), keeps a high-water mark in `rider_progress`, and returns level, XP, credits and owned items. `my_wallet()`, `buy_item(p_item)`, `my_challenges()` and `claim_challenge(p_id)` are the public entry points (security definer, `authenticated` only). `store_items`, `purchases`, `challenge_claims` and `rider_progress` have RLS and no policies.
- `public.store_items` is seeded from `src/garage/catalogue.ts`: run `node scripts/gen-store-sql.mjs` after changing the catalogue and apply the printed SQL. The effects there are informational: the app applies `catalogue.ts`.
- **Effects** live in one place: `main.ts`, "Garage equipment", next to `stepSpeed()`: `massKg + equipment.massKg`, `cda * equipment.cdaFactor`, `crr + equipment.crrDelta`. `garage/effects.ts` reuses the same maths to describe items.
- **Loadout**: `settings.loadout` (item id per slot), validated by `cleanLoadout()` against the wallet's owned list (starters are always owned; disc wheels need the time-trial bike). `Garage.apply()` pushes the style into `kit.ts` (`setPlayerStyle`), which sends it to the world and through the kit string.
- **Kit string**: `#jersey,#accent,#frame` then optional `;f=tron;w=deep80;t=race;h=aero;p=stars;s=neon` (`garage/style.ts`). `ride_presence()` validates it with a regex; old clients keep sending three colours.
- **Rendering**: `RiderModel.setLook({ style })` rebuilds the frame, wheels and bars (`buildFrame()`); `world/bikeParts.ts` has the wheel builder, Tron materials and `updateGlow(time, night)` (called by the world each frame; neon materials are `toneMapped: false`), helmet shells (attached to the rig's Head bone, in centimetres) and `applyKitStyle()` (the shader recolours navy and red, draws patterns from the pre-skinning `position`, and recolours white near the ankles into socks). A time-trial bike sets `Rig.tt` for a flatter back and hands on the extensions.
- **Weekly challenges**: the pool and the pick (lowest `md5(week || id)` per family) exist in SQL and in `garage/challenges.ts`; a test pins them together. Race wins and podiums use `activities.race_place`, pro-team riding uses `activities.pro_m` (metres within 30 m of a pro rider, counted in `main.ts`).
- **Share card**: `garage/shareCard.ts` draws on a canvas; `shareUi.ts` builds the data from a live ride or a saved activity (routes known only for built-in stages).
