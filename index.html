Welcome to the free American Flyers Training Program

Take on the Hell of the West, the final stage from the film, then Alpe d'Huez, Mont Ventoux and more in 3D on your Wahoo or any smart trainer. Ride with friends, race your best ride's ghost, and follow a training plan, right in your browser.

Free to use: no subscription, no card. Signing in saves your rides and plans, and puts you on the road with other riders and on the leaderboards.
